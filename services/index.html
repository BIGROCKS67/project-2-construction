Our services

Whether you are adding space, reworking the house you already have or starting from scratch, we can take on the building work and see it through to the finish. We work for homeowners and commercial clients, and we are always happy to talk through what your project needs.

New builds and extensions

Building an extension is about more than adding space. It needs to feel like a natural part of the property and work for the people who use it. From garages and extensions to new builds, we take on residential and commercial projects with care and attention to detail.

Renovations and conversions

Sometimes the building is right but the layout isn’t. We open up rooms, move walls, change layouts and convert spaces so an existing property works better for the way you live or use it, and we have put a flood-damaged home back together from the bare walls up.

Kitchens and bathrooms

A kitchen or bathroom brings a lot of trades into one room. We fit the units, sanitaryware and bespoke joinery, and coordinate the plumbing, electrics and tiling around them, so the finished room comes together properly.

Windows and doors

New windows and doors make a big difference to how a property looks and feels. We fit them on new builds, extensions and renovations, and as replacements, and we are happy to talk through the options that suit your property.

FENSA Approved Installer

Project 2 Construction is a FENSA Approved Installer. When we replace windows or doors in your home, we register the work with FENSA and you receive a FENSA certificate showing it meets Building Regulations.
