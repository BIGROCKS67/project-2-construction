Privacy notice

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Project 2 Construction is the trading name of Project 2 Construction Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company no. 14410388. Registered office: 11b Boundary Road, Brackley, Northants, NN13 7ES. We are the controller of the personal details you give us.

To ask about your details, email info@project2construction.com or call 07921 817690.

What we collect and why

Enquiries. When you call or email us about a project, we keep your name, contact details, the property address and anything you send us about the work, such as drawings or photos. We use this to reply, visit, price the job and, if you go ahead, to carry out the work.

Job applications. When you apply for a job, we keep your name, contact details, CV, portfolio, references and anything else you send, such as driving licence details. We use this only to consider you for work with us.

This website does not have a contact form. It does not ask you for any details.

Our lawful basis

For enquiries and job applications, we rely on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. If you become a customer or join the team, we use your details to carry out that contract. We also have a legitimate interest in keeping simple records of our work, and we keep some records because the law requires it, for example for tax.

Who receives your details

We do not sell your details or use them for marketing. The services that handle them for us are:

  • Microsoft 365, which runs our email.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which hosts this website and keeps short technical logs of visits, including IP addresses.

We may also share project details with people working on your job, such as architects, suppliers or building control, where that is needed for the work.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of these services may store or process data in the USA or elsewhere outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contract clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years, unless you become a customer. Customer records are kept for as long as we need them for the job, any guarantee, and tax and legal records, usually up to 7 years after the work ends. Unsuccessful job applications are deleted within 6 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your details, and ask us to correct them, delete them, restrict how we use them, or stop using them. You can also ask for them in a form you can pass to someone else. Email or call us using the details above. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to put it right first.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. It has no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and it does not load maps, videos or social media from other sites. Links to Facebook only open Facebook if you click them.
