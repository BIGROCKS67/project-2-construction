The project

In Launton we added a rear extension and changed the layout inside, so the house makes better use of its space.

What we did

The job started with the groundworks, digging out and laying the foundations. The extension walls and roof structure followed. Inside, we fitted a new shaker kitchen with quartz worktops, tiled and fitted out the bathroom and a walk-in shower room, and hung new doors throughout, including black-framed glazed doors off the hallway.
