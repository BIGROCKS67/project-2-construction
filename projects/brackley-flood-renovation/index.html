The project

After flooding left this Brackley house badly damaged, it needed far more than a tidy-up. We started with a full strip-out, then put the house back together room by room, from fresh plaster and new floors to refitted bathrooms.

What we did

Every room was replastered and decorated, with new woodwork and oak doors throughout. We fitted new sockets and switches and swapped the pendant lights for spotlights. Herringbone flooring now runs through the hallway, kitchen, utility, WC and office, with carpet on the stairs, landing and bedroom. In the utility, new units and an oak worktop sit around the customer’s own Belfast sink, which we kept and reused. The staircase has a new oak handrail and spindles, with the newel posts clad in oak, and the en suite, bathroom and WC all have new sanitaryware and tiling.
